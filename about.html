OUR STORY

A New Identity. Built on Two Decades of Experience.

Recruitment Beyond Resumes

For more than two decades, I have worked with people, organisations, leaders and talent — building, learning and growing through experiences that shaped not just how I work, but what I believe good HR and recruitment should look like.

For many years, I was building that work under an identity that was not my own.

The work was mine.
The experience was mine.
The relationships I built were mine.
The lessons — both the successes and the difficult ones — were mine.

But the identity belonged elsewhere.

And somewhere along the way, I realised something important:

I didn't want to simply continue doing the work. I wanted to build something that carried my own values, my own perspective and my own way of working.

That realisation became the beginning of something I had wanted for a long time — the freedom to build something of my own.

That something became GuideNX.

GuideNX is not a reinvention of what I know. It is everything I have learned, experienced and built over the years — now brought together under my own identity, values and vision.

What changes is the name. What changes is the freedom to build it my way.

And that is why GuideNX is more than a new business for me. It is a new chapter of work that has been years in the making.

New Brand. Not New to the Work.

GuideNX Search may be a new name in the market, but the work behind it isn't new. The experience, judgement and understanding that shape every search have been built over more than two decades of working across HR, recruitment, leadership and talent.

I am not starting from zero. I am starting from experience.

Looking Beyond the Resume

My approach to recruitment has always been shaped by a belief that people cannot be understood through a resume alone.

My experience in Strategic Human Resources, together with my background in counselling and psychometric assessment, has taught me to look beyond qualifications and years of experience — towards capability, behavioural fit, motivation and the environment in which someone can truly thrive.

That perspective now sits at the heart of GuideNX Search.

We work across the recruitment spectrum — from blue-collar and volume hiring to specialist, niche and leadership roles.

Because regardless of the level of the role, the principle remains the same: find the person who is right for the role, right for the organisation, and right for what comes next.
